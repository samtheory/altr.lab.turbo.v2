## Usage

One side is the action. The other side can be an icon, a count, or any other widget. `ButtonGroupSeparator` draws the dividing line. Leave it out and the segments sit flush.

```tsx
<ButtonGroup>
  <ButtonGroupItem>
    <Star />
    Star
  </ButtonGroupItem>
  <ButtonGroupSeparator />
  <ButtonGroupAddon>
    <Github />
    15K
  </ButtonGroupAddon>
</ButtonGroup>
```

`variant="default"` paints the whole group with the primary face. The line uses the primary foreground at low opacity. `variant="outline"` is the neutral face.

## Accessibility

- The group is `role="group"`. Give it an `aria-label` when the visible text is not enough.
- `ButtonGroupItem` is a real button. The addon is not, unless you put a button inside it.
- The separator is decorative.
