## Usage

```tsx
<MegaNav>
  <MegaNavList>
    <MegaNavItem value="products">
      <MegaNavTrigger>Products</MegaNavTrigger>
      <MegaNavPanel>
        <MegaNavLink href="/kit" title="Kit" description="Primitives and tokens." />
      </MegaNavPanel>
    </MegaNavItem>
    <li>
      <MegaNavAnchor href="/pricing">Pricing</MegaNavAnchor>
    </li>
  </MegaNavList>
</MegaNav>
```

Hover or focus a trigger to open its panel. `Escape` closes. Keyboard: `ArrowDown` opens the focused item.
