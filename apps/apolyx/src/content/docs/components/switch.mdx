## Physics

- **Press** — the thumb stretches toward the side it is about to travel to.
- **Drag** — grab the thumb and slide it; past the midpoint on release it commits, otherwise it snaps back. Pulling past a rail stretches it against the wall (rubber band).
- **Release** — the thumb springs to the end, squashes a little on impact, and settles back to its normal width.
- **Reduced motion** — no stretch or bounce; the thumb moves straight to its end.

Click, Space, and Enter still toggle exactly once.

## Accessibility

`role="switch"` with `aria-checked`. Space and Enter toggle. Use a switch for settings that apply immediately; use a checkbox inside forms that are submitted.
