## Usage

```tsx
<SwitchField
  label="Background sync"
  description="Keeps drafts up to date."
  checked={sync}
  onCheckedChange={setSync}
/>
```

Use `Switch` on its own when you place the label yourself, `SwitchField` for a labelled settings row, and `SwitchCard` for a selectable tile.

## Physics

The thumb geometry is a pure function, `computeThumb`, so every limit below is unit-tested.

- **Press** — the thumb stretches toward the side it will travel to by 20% of its width, over about 120 ms. Nothing changes on the frame you press.
- **Drag** — the thumb follows the pointer. Stretch comes from smoothed pointer speed, leads in the direction of motion, and relaxes when you stop. It never exceeds **30% of the thumb width**.
- **Ends** — stretch tapers to zero over the last half-thumb, so the thumb arrives at its normal size. Pushing past an end, or into the side it already rests on, never grows it: it squashes by at most 2 px against the wall.
- **Direction changes** — need 2 px of movement the other way, so jitter does not flip the stretch.
- **Release** — the thumb glides home with a near-critically damped spring and lands with a 1.5 px squash. Past the midpoint commits; under 3 px of movement counts as a click.
- **Track tint** — while dragging, the track colour blends from the off colour to the tone by progress.
- **Reduced motion** — no stretch, squash or tint animation; the thumb moves with a short tween.

## Accessibility

- `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`. Space and Enter toggle exactly once; so does a click.
- `SwitchField` and `SwitchCard` render a `<label>`, so clicking anywhere on the row or card toggles. The switch is named by the label text (`aria-labelledby`) and described by the description (`aria-describedby`).
- `FieldSet` is a native fieldset: screen readers announce the legend as the group name.
- Use a switch for settings that apply immediately; use a checkbox in forms that are submitted later.
