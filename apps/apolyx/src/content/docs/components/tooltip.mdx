## Usage

```tsx
<TooltipProvider>
  <Tooltip>
    <TooltipTrigger asChild>
      <Button size="icon" aria-label="Bold">
        <Bold />
      </Button>
    </TooltipTrigger>
    <TooltipContent>Bold</TooltipContent>
  </Tooltip>
</TooltipProvider>
```

Put one `TooltipProvider` near the root. After one tooltip closes, the next opens without the delay for a short window, so scanning a toolbar feels instant.

## Accessibility

- Opens on hover after the delay and immediately on keyboard focus; closes on leave, blur, pointer down, and Escape.
- The trigger gets `aria-describedby` pointing at `role="tooltip"` content.
- Tooltips are hints — never put information or actions only in a tooltip, and do not open them on touch.
