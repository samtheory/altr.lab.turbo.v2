## Usage

```tsx
<NavigationMenu>
  <NavigationMenuList>
    <NavigationMenuItem value="kit">
      <NavigationMenuTrigger>Kit</NavigationMenuTrigger>
      <NavigationMenuContent>
        <NavigationMenuPanelList>
          <li><NavigationMenuLink href="/docs/components/button">Components</NavigationMenuLink></li>
        </NavigationMenuPanelList>
      </NavigationMenuContent>
    </NavigationMenuItem>
    <NavigationMenuItem>
      <NavigationMenuLink href="/docs" active>Docs</NavigationMenuLink>
    </NavigationMenuItem>
  </NavigationMenuList>
</NavigationMenu>
```

## Behaviour

- Panels open on hover after `openDelay` and close after `closeDelay`, so moving the pointer diagonally into a panel does not flicker.
- Click toggles a panel; only one panel is open at a time.
- Panels render inline (not portaled) and fade and drop in with Motion.

## Accessibility

- Triggers expose `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- ArrowDown on a trigger opens its panel; Left / Right move between top-level items.
- Escape and outside click close the panel and return focus to its trigger.
- Active links use `aria-current="page"`.
