## Usage

```tsx
<ForgeLoader title="Forging image" duration={4800} />
```

Omit `value` for auto progress, or pass `value={0…100}` for controlled progress. Status lines cycle while loading; motion and the forge sheen pause under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
